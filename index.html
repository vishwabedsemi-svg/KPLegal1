05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Not sure where to begin? Here are a few useful starting points.

Ask about your situation ↗
What types of matters can a paralegal handle in Ontario?+

Ontario paralegals may provide legal services only within the scope authorized by the Law Society of Ontario and their licence. The permitted scope depends on the type of matter and proceeding. We will confirm whether your matter is suitable before accepting it.

How do I know if you can help with my matter?+

Start with a brief, non-confidential overview and any key deadline. We can discuss whether the issue appears to fall within our permitted scope, whether a conflict check is needed and what information would be required next.

How do fees work?+

Fees depend on the nature and complexity of the matter and the work requested. Before an engagement begins, the scope, fees and applicable terms should be discussed and documented clearly.

Does sending a message make you my representative?+

No. Contacting us through this website does not create a paralegal-client relationship. Do not send sensitive or confidential information until we confirm how it can be received securely and agree to act for you.
